Support

Getting help

Email support@maxpetrov.pp.ua — the link pre-fills the subject [Issue Tree Templates for Jira] Support request.

What to include

  • The site URL where you saw the issue

  • The app's version, visible on the Manage apps page

  • The exact steps you took

  • A screenshot of what you saw

Hours and response time

Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 Kyiv time (GMT+3). One person reads that mailbox — the one who wrote the app — and every request gets a first reply within two business days. That is the window a part-time maintainer can hold every week rather than on a good one; most replies land sooner.
